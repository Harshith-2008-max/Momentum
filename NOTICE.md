## NASA open data used at runtime (via api.nasa.gov and images-api.nasa.gov)
- NASA Image and Video Library API: videos shown in training steps, the Watch tab and mission debriefs. Each video remains the property of its credited source; follow NASA's media usage guidelines.
- NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) API: the home-screen picture, credited in the app as "NASA Picture of the Day".
- NASA DONKI space-weather database (flares): the strongest solar flare of the last 30 days sets the in-game storm.

## Facts built into the simulation (please verify against the originals before citing)
- Moon surface radiation about 1.37 mSv/day: Zhang et al., Science Advances, 2020 (Chang'e-4 lander).
- Mars surface radiation about 0.64 mSv/day: Hassler et al., Science, 2014 (Curiosity RAD).
- Mars receives about 43% of the sunlight Earth does: NASA Mars facts.
- ISS water recovery about 98%: NASA, 2023.
- NASA career radiation limit 600 mSv: NASA standard, 2022.
- MOXIE oxygen from Martian carbon dioxide: NASA Perseverance mission.
- Opportunity rover and the 2018 global dust storm: NASA/JPL.
- Chandrayaan-3 lander Vikram and rover Pragyan, lunar night 2023: ISRO.
- August 1972 solar storm between Apollo 16 and 17: NASA.
The game scales and simplifies these into teaching values; it is not a mission-planning tool.
